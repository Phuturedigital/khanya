About Khanya

Excellence in dentistry with compassionate care

Khanya means to shine, to give light in isiXhosa and isiZulu. It is the right word for a dental practice: the work is quiet, technical and largely invisible, and what people actually notice is the confidence afterwards.

The concept imagines a Sea Point practice built around three commitments — a quoted price before treatment begins, appointments that run to time, and plain-language explanations of every procedure. Medical aid claims submitted directly where the scheme allows.
