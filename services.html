Everyday dentistry, done properly

General, hygiene and cosmetic dentistry under one roof in Sea Point. Every plan is quoted before treatment starts, so you know the number before you are in the chair.

General

Keeping teeth healthy

Check-ups & X-rays

A full examination every six months, with digital X-rays when they are clinically indicated rather than by default. You get shown what we see.

Scale & polish

Hygienist-led cleaning to remove plaque and tartar, plus a practical brushing and flossing plan for the gaps your technique keeps missing.

White fillings

Tooth-coloured composite rather than amalgam, matched to the shade of the surrounding tooth so the repair is not the first thing anyone notices.

Gum care

Treatment for bleeding, receding or inflamed gums, including deep cleaning below the gumline and a maintenance interval that suits your risk.

Retainers & mouthguards

Custom retainers after orthodontic work, night guards for grinding, and sports guards for rugby, hockey and boxing.

Emergency appointments

Slots held open each weekday for pain, swelling, a broken tooth or a lost filling. Ring first — the concept keeps two same-day slots back.

Cosmetic

Work you choose, not work you need

Cosmetic dentistry is elective. It is quoted separately, it is rarely covered by a medical aid, and nobody at Khanya would push it on you during a check-up.

Whitening

In-chair whitening in a single visit, or a take-home tray system over two weeks. Results depend heavily on the starting shade and on whether the discolouration is on the surface or inside the tooth — which is something to establish before you pay, not after.

Veneers & bonding

Composite bonding for chips and small gaps, or porcelain veneers where the change is larger. Porcelain is irreversible: enamel is removed and does not grow back. The concept practice would say so at the consultation.
