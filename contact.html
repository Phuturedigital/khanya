Questions

Before you ask

Is Khanya Dental Studio a real practice?

No. It is an invented brand created by Phuture Digital to demonstrate a website design. There is no practice, no practitioners, no HPCSA registration and no premises. Nothing on this site should inform a decision about dental care.

Are the people in the photographs Khanya staff?

No. Every photograph is licensed stock and everyone shown is a model. No real person on this site has been given a clinical credential, a name or a quote.

Are the prices real?

No. They are invented placeholder figures chosen to look plausible so the layout can be judged. They are not benchmarked against any tariff guide and no practice stands behind them.

What happens if I submit the form?

Nothing is sent anywhere. The page tells you plainly that no appointment was booked and offers to open a pre-filled email to Phuture Digital instead. There is no server, so your details are never transmitted or stored.

Can I have a site like this for my practice?

Yes — that is what the concept is for. Email hello@phuturedigital.co.za and it would be built with your real brand, your real team and your real fees.
